# Milestone 9 — integrated local recovery QA

Owner: Bernie. Date: 2026-10-12. Scope: existing providers only, tickets 1–7.

## Implementation

Tickets 4–7 add explicit integration/source routes, logical-message/attempt
identity, encrypted opt-in queue ownership, bounded safe retries/failover,
operator controls, audit, lifecycle and retention. See ADRs 0034–0037. Recovery
and routing are off by default; no real provider credentials were changed or
email sent during these checks.

Ticket checkpoints are stacked locally on tickets 1–3. The combined worktree
also includes preserved pre-existing Milestone 8 failure-category and
deliverability changes. Shared wiring, schema gates, provider safety evidence,
retention and fixture changes are intentionally not bundled into checkpoint
commits. Checkpoints are not standalone deployable PRs. No push, remote CI,
merge, release package or staging deployment occurred for this work.
